Before you start — what SlipDesk is

SlipDesk prepares renewal paperwork. It does not sign, file, or submit anything. SlipDesk assembles each slip's renewal contract from the marina's own records, tracks insurance certificates and boat registrations, chases the boaters who haven't answered, works the waitlist, and produces the invoice list for your bookkeeping.

The marina reviews and sends every contract. Each boater reviews and signs their own renewal — SlipDesk never signs for a boater, and a signature recorded in this demo is a simulation of the boater's own action on their no-login link.

Insurance and registration requirements shown in SlipDesk are your marina's own policy settings, entered by you — not legal advice and not a statement of any law. Check requirements with your insurer and your state boating authority.

    How a renewal flows, start to finish

    1) The slip sits at Not started. 2) From the slip's file, open the boater link preview and mark the link sent — the slip becomes Invited and the chase clock starts. 3) The boater reviews, adds their insurance certificate and registration on their own link, and signs — the slip becomes Signed. 4) When the certificate and registration pass your policy settings and the deposit is in, the slip shows Renewed. Nothing is signed by SlipDesk at any step: the boater signs their own renewal, and you review it.

    What counts as an outstanding insurance certificate?

    Three things, all judged against your settings: no certificate on file, a certificate that expires before the season starts, or a liability limit below the minimum set in Settings. SlipDesk does not know the law on this — the minimum is your marina's policy, so confirm it with your insurance agent (see the VERIFY note in Settings).

    How does the chase queue decide who is first?

    By days waiting since the last touch — link sent, a logged contact, or a signature — longest first. Each contact you log restarts that slip's clock. The suggested step follows your ladder: link, first nudge, second nudge, final notice. After the final notice you decide whether to release the slip to the waitlist; SlipDesk never releases a slip on its own.

    How do waitlist offers work?

    On a declined or released slip, open the Waitlist and offer it to the first waiting boater whose boat fits. The offer holds for the window set in Settings (7 days in this demo), then expires by itself. If the boater accepts, they become the slip's holder at Not started and their renewal begins.

    Where does my data live? How do I back it up?

    Your data lives in this browser on this device — SlipDesk has no account and no server, and this demo transmits nothing. Back it up from Export → Download backup (JSON), and restore from the same screen. Do the backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.

    Can I talk instead of typing?

    Yes — every entry field has a microphone button. One tap and the app is listening; what you say lands in the field for you to check before saving, and nothing sends itself. Voice entry needs a browser that supports it (hosted versions); where it isn't available the button tells you plainly and typing works as always.
